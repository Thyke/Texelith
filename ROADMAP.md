# Roadmap

## Completed

- Phase 0: workspace foundation, GPU compute, image I/O, commands and undo.
- Phase 1: material maps, seamless processing, 3D preview, export presets, projects and batch processing.
- Phase 1.5: interface and workflow improvements.

## Planned: Phase 2 — Layer & Paint

- Layer stacks and colour painting.
- Clone and heal across maps, decals and texture placement.
- Normal editing and AI-assisted layers.

## Later ideas

The product specification also describes AI material estimation, de-lighting, upscaling and masking; CLI and MCP automation; compression preview and validation. These are ideas for future work, not a release schedule.
